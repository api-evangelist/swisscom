In the current financial and regulatory landscape, institutions face mounting pressure to navigate complex compliance demands while simultaneously delivering seamless digital experiences to customers. The tension between robust anti-money laundering (AML) controls and user-friendly onboarding isn't theoretical: the consequences are real, and the costs are high. According to Signicat, 68% of consumers in Europe have already abandoned financial service applications at some point due to onboarding difficulties.
