The European mobility as a service (MaaS) market, which encompasses flexible models such as car leasing, renting, sharing, and subscriptions, is experiencing significant growth. Leasing is becoming increasingly common among individuals considering the purchase of a new vehicle. Especially in large cities, it is less attractive for residents to own their own car, so they turn to car sharing or subscriptions instead.
