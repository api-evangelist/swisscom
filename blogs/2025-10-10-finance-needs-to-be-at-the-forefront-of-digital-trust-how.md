With recent developments like VR, AI, and metaverse environments, the boundaries between the physical world and people's digital representations are becoming increasingly blurred. On the one hand, this enables truly immersive experiences, but on the other hand, it also creates new challenges and threats. This applies above all to the financial sector, where transactions involving highly sensitive data and significant risk potential are carried out digitally.
