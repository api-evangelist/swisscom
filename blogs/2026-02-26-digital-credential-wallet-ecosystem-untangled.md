How to benefit from digital credentials without losing your mind over regulations and technical standards Digital credential wallets are moving from vision to reality. With initiatives such as Switzerland’s national wallet (SWIYU) and the European Digital Identity Wallet (EUDI, eIDAS2), a new infrastructure for trusted digital interactions is emerging across public and private sectors. At its core, this shift changes the nature of digital data.
