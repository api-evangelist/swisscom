Digital credential wallets are moving from concept to reality. The EU has set a deadline for member states to introduce an application by the end of this year, and in Switzerland, the introduction of an E-ID system with the national wallet, swiyu, was announced recently for the 1st of December 2026. With the introduction of these identity frameworks, businesses must now evaluate how these wallets impact their operations, customer experience, and competitive positioning.
